Commit One

A blank page waits inside the folder,
a quiet room with nothing said.
I write a line, I save the moment,
and Git remembers what I wrote.

If I change my mind tomorrow,
the old words are never lost:
every version waits in history,
a small door back to what I wrote.

So I branch to try new verses,
leave the first draft safe at home,
and bring the good ones back to main
when I am sure they belong.
